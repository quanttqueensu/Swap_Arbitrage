# P34 costs and portfolio verification

## Scope, versions, and boundaries

P34 publishes the existing pure cost and portfolio APIs at the strategy
boundary: `CostEstimate`, `naive_cost`, `observed_cost`, `portfolio_dv01`, and
`select_portfolio_targets`. It composes them with P31
`p10.strategy-equations.v1`, P32 ranking, and P33
`p33.position-sizing-risk.v1`; the verification runtime was Python `3.12.13`.
No equation, dependency, broker, file, clock, network, or backtest path was
added. The frozen fixture read for verification was
`docs/tests/fixtures/strategy_equation_examples.json`, SHA-256
`3FB9DA5FC9AD255587CE93EA9770552F42566D56070F68AD1661709C030FBD76`.

## Public contract and hand calculation

The public cost record keeps itemized USD components, total USD cost, and total
cost bps. `observed_cost` accepts only caller-supplied causal directional USD
inputs for the proposed trade; it neither estimates nor defaults them. Missing,
nonfinite, or invalid components and a missing/nonpositive USD/bp base return
no result. The explicit 2Y literals are swap `250`, Treasury `250`,
commission/exchange `100`, slippage `200`, roll close/open `50`/`50`, and
financing not already included in funding `100` USD. Their total is
`1000` USD; divided by the `1000` USD/bp base, the cost is `1` bp. P31
traditional/reverse net opportunities are `25 - 1 = 24` bp and
`-25 - 1 = -26` bp. The real `observed_cost` 5Y inputs `600`, `600`, `400`,
`800`, `200`, `200`, and `200` USD total `3000`; its `3` bp buffer makes P31
traditional/reverse nets `15 - 3 = 12` bp and `-15 - 3 = -18` bp. The pure
flow places each calculated `CostEstimate.total_cost_bps` in both matching
`SpreadObservation` directional cost-buffer fields and derives both directional
nets with `net_opportunity_bps` from that maturity's literal gross and cost.

P32 ranks the literal eligible rows as `("5Y", "2Y")`. Each real P33 target
uses a `1000` USD/bp base target, swap/Treasury DV01 `100`/`950`, and has gross
DV01 `1950` and residual net DV01 `-50` USD/bp. Rank-first selection under
gross/net limits `5000`/`250` selects `("5Y", "2Y")`; direct aggregation is
gross `3900` and net `-100` USD/bp. Existing P33 risk inputs, including
residual fraction and limit both `0.05`, produce an allowed decision.

Selection is conservative: it walks the supplied P32 order, admits a target
only when candidate gross is within the gross limit and absolute candidate net
is within the net limit, skips breaches, and continues lower ranks. It never
re-ranks, optimizes offsets, or fills malformed/missing inputs.
