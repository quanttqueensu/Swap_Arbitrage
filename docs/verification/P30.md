# P30 strategy interface verification

## Scope and non-goals

P30 defines immutable, validated strategy-boundary records and deterministic
CSV-row serialization in `strategy.models`. It does not implement strategy
equations, rolling signals, position construction, risk policy, broker
adapters, data reads, migration, clocks, networks, or behavior migration.

## Domains and validation

- `PositionState` and `TradeDirection`: `REVERSE=-1`, `FLAT=0`,
  `TRADITIONAL=1`.
- `OrderSide`: `BUY="BUY"`, `SELL="SELL"`; `OrderType`: `MARKET="MKT"`,
  `LIMIT="LMT"`; `TimeInForce`: `DAY="DAY"`; `FlattenUrgency`:
  `NONE="none"`, `SCHEDULED="scheduled"`, `EMERGENCY="emergency"`.
- Required text is exact `str` and nonblank. Datetimes are exact,
  timezone-aware UTC `datetime` values. Decimals are exact, finite `Decimal`
  values. Integer fields are exact `int`; boolean flags are exact `bool`.
- `ContractMetadata.rate_sensitivity_sign` is an exact `int` and must be one
  of `-1` or `1`.
- Availability cannot precede observation; snapshot observations must be
  available at its decision time; intent times satisfy earliest ≤ activation ≤
  expiry. Snapshot and output collections normalize to tuples and require their
  declared concrete members.
- Price and contract DV01 are positive. Required absolute amounts, buffers,
  counts, costs, slippage, target/gross DV01, and working-order quantities are
  nonnegative. Signed positions, quantities, opportunities, and residual net
  DV01 may be negative. `z_score` alone may be `None`. Risk scale is in
  `[0, 1]`; reason codes are nonblank and unique; `paper_only is True`.

## Literal construction and serialization examples

```python
UTC = timezone.utc
NOW = datetime(2026, 8, 3, 21, tzinfo=UTC)
decision = SignalDecision(
    decision_id="decision-1", maturity="2Y", decision_time_utc=NOW,
    prior_state=PositionState.FLAT, new_state=PositionState.TRADITIONAL,
    direction=TradeDirection.TRADITIONAL, reason_code="entry_threshold",
    feature_values=(NamedValue("z_score", Decimal("1.75"), "standard_deviations"),),
    strategy_version="swap-arb-1", configuration_version="config-1",
)
intent = OrderIntent(
    run_id="run-1", agent_id="agent-0", strategy_id="swap-arb",
    decision_id="decision-1", instrument_id="ERIS-YIT", side=OrderSide.SELL,
    quantity_contracts=3, order_type=OrderType.MARKET,
    time_in_force=TimeInForce.DAY, earliest_submission_utc=NOW,
    activate_at_utc=NOW + timedelta(minutes=1),
    expires_at_utc=NOW + timedelta(hours=1),
    reference_price_points=Decimal("99.25"),
    max_slippage_price_points=Decimal("0.05"), paper_only=True,
)
to_csv_row(decision)
# {
#   "decision_id": "decision-1", "maturity": "2Y",
#   "decision_time_utc": "2026-08-03T21:00:00Z", "prior_state": "0",
#   "new_state": "1", "direction": "1", "reason_code": "entry_threshold",
#   "feature_values": "[{\"name\":\"z_score\",\"value\":\"1.75\",\"unit\":\"standard_deviations\"}]",
#   "strategy_version": "swap-arb-1", "configuration_version": "config-1"
# }
```

`to_csv_row(record: object) -> dict[str, str]` accepts dataclass instances
only, returns a fresh declaration-ordered dict, and does not mutate its input.
Enums serialize before scalar handling; booleans are lowercase; `None` is
empty; UTC datetimes end in `Z`; finite Decimals use fixed-point notation; and
nested dataclasses/tuples become compact JSON.
