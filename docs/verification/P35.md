## Exact blockers and policy conflict

1. The required P11 source-coverage matrix is absent; `P31.md` independently
   records it as unavailable.
2. There is no `contract_reference/contracts.csv`, paper quote/liquidity
   partition, active executable contract pair, multiplier mapping, or
   maturity-specific roll rule.
3. The available market Treasury legs are continuous Yahoo proxies, and the
   available swap legs are price points rather than canonical swap rates.
   Contract-risk values cannot close this gap because their exact Eris or
   fixed-ICS-proxy lineage cannot be tied to active reference contracts.
4. The golden fixture contains only 2Y/5Y equation examples; it has no 10Y or
   30Y mapping and no maturity-integrated data/contract proof.
5. P35 asks for canonical-data manifests, but P24's approved layout expressly
   removed durable manifests and staging. `docs/data/canonical-schemas.md` and
   `docs/data/migration-preview.md` make immutable Git history and source
   fields the provenance mechanism. This conflict cannot be resolved by
   fabricating a manifest.
6. MG4 and MG5 are not started, so no maturity can satisfy the required
   MG2-through-MG5 chain.

`complete_2y_5y` remains reserved by the master plan but is not emitted: even
2Y and 5Y fail P35. No substitute scope label is created.
